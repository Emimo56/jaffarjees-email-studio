# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

@brand/voice.md

## Folders

- **brand/** — Brand voice, logo, colors, and style notes to keep emails on-brand.
- **leads/** — Lists and notes about people and businesses we might email.
- **campaigns/** — Drafts and plans for specific email campaigns.
- **notes/** — General notes, ideas, and things to follow up on.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. No jargon, no corporate-speak.
- Never send email to real people. Drafts and plans only. The one exception: sending through the Resend MCP connection is allowed only to Resend's own test addresses (ending in @resend.dev) and to my own email address — a guard will enforce this limit in the next step.
- Ask before deleting anything.
