# Site Inconsistencies & Things to Watch

Checked live on usa.jafferjees.com, 2026-11-08, via direct browser navigation (not just research-agent summaries — each item below was verified by loading the page and screenshotting it). Keep adding to this file as more are found. These are flags for whoever maintains the live site and for us, so our own copy doesn't repeat an error or an unconfirmed claim.

## 1. "Over 140 years" vs. "Since 1880"

The About Us page title tag says **"About Us | Jafferjees – Luxury Leather Goods Since 1880"**, but the body copy says **"built on a legacy of quality craftsmanship for over 140 years."** 2026 − 1880 = 146 years, so "over 140" is technically true but is already stale and will keep drifting further from "since 1880" every year it isn't updated.

- Screenshot: [about-us-140-years-vs-1880.jpg](./about-us-140-years-vs-1880.jpg)
- URL: https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/about-us

**For our own copy**: use "since 1880" / "fifth generation" — don't repeat "140 years," since it's already a rounding-down and not a stable number to anchor on.

## 2. "Jaffeejees" misspelling (blog post)

One blog post's visible H1 heading and URL slug misspell the brand name as "Jaffeejees" instead of "Jafferjees." The rest of the same page (logo alt text, copyright line, body text) spells the brand correctly — this typo is isolated to one post's heading/slug, not a site-wide branding issue.

- Screenshot: [blog-jaffeejees-typo.jpg](./blog-jaffeejees-typo.jpg)
- URL: https://usa.jafferjees.com/blogs/news/comfortable-leather-bags-by-jaffeejees

**Action**: worth flagging to whoever maintains the live site (title and URL slug both need fixing — changing the slug would also need a redirect to avoid breaking the existing link). Not a voice issue, just a typo.

## 3. Discounts/sales — needs re-checking before any email goes out

- `/collections/sale` returns a genuine **404** (confirmed by direct navigation, not just a rate-limit).
- A site search for "sale" returns 254 results, but every item shown lists only a "Regular price" — no struck-through/discounted prices were visible on the results checked.
- Screenshot: [homepage-no-discount-banner.jpg](./homepage-no-discount-banner.jpg) (homepage, no promo banner at time of check)

**This is current-state, not permanent.** Jafferjees could run a sale at any time (seasonal, clearance, etc.) without us knowing. **Before any campaign or email references pricing, discounts, or "no sale" as a fact, re-check the live site first** — don't assume this snapshot still holds.

## 4. Country of manufacture: now confirmed

Earlier research couldn't find a stated country of origin on the site. The user has since confirmed directly: **products are made in Pakistan**, and sold in Pakistan, UK, and USA. The homepage title tag ("...in Pakistan, UK & USA") was ambiguous about this — it names markets, not manufacturing origin — so this fact had to come from the user, not the site. Safe to state "made in Pakistan" in our own copy; the site itself still doesn't say this anywhere, so don't cite the website as the source if asked.

## Other things to be careful of

- **No stated warranty/guarantee anywhere on the live site** (confirmed directly, not just absence-by-omission) — don't imply one.
- **No loyalty, referral, or affiliate program** exists anywhere on the site, including footer links — don't imply one.
- **No certification/authenticity claims** (no "genuine leather" certification, no cruelty-free or third-party eco-certification language) — the only ethics-adjacent language is self-described ("source leather responsibly"), not third-party verified. Don't upgrade this to a certification claim in our copy.
- **No press/"as seen in" mentions or trust badges** anywhere on the site — don't invent any.
- **No competitor names or comparisons** anywhere on the site — the "Compare" feature exists but is an empty, generic product-compare tool, not a competitor callout.
- **"Handmade" never appears** — the site consistently uses "handcrafted." Treat these as not interchangeable in our own copy.
