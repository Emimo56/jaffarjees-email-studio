# Words Competitors Use That Jafferjees Doesn't

Pulled from [`notes/competitive-brief-leather-goods.md`](../notes/competitive-brief-leather-goods.md) and cross-checked against [`notes/competitive-brief-fact-check.md`](../notes/competitive-brief-fact-check.md). These are words/phrases that show up in competitor messaging — and are *true, usable claims for Jafferjees* (not generic retail jargon like "curated" or "elevate," which the voice guide already bans) — that Jafferjees' own materials don't currently use.

|---|---|---|---|
| "Passed down" / "built to be passed down" | Shinola ("purpose-built to be passed down") | Jafferjees' real multi-generational history earns this more honestly than Shinola's 2011-founded brand | [Shinola positioning, competitive-brief-leather-goods.md §Shinola](../notes/competitive-brief-leather-goods.md) |
| "Fewer, better things" (concept, not verbatim — needs own phrasing) | Will Leather Goods, Bosca, Cuyana all lean on this idea | Supports the "not cheap at all" positioning without sounding like a discount pitch | [competitive-brief-leather-goods.md §2](../notes/competitive-brief-leather-goods.md) |

## Words NOT recommended (already banned or risky for Jafferjees)

- **"Curated," "elevate," "unlock"** — already banned in [`brand/voice.md`](../brand/voice.md) as retail jargon.
- **"Guarantee" / "warranty" (unqualified)** — competitors use this heavily (Saddleback's 100-year, Will's lifetime), but Jafferjees has no stated warranty today. Using the word without a real, published policy behind it would be a false claim — flag as a product/policy decision, not a copy fix.
- **"Lifetime"** — same issue: don't borrow the word until there's a real policy to back it.

## How this maps to the voice guide

These are candidates for the "words we use" section — none of them conflict with the no-refund policy, delivery-date rule, or the no-affiliate-program rule. The three flagged above should stay out until Jafferjees actually publishes a warranty/guarantee policy.
