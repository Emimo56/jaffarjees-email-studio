# Jafferjees' Own Vocabulary (from usa.jafferjees.com)

Pulled by three parallel research passes over usa.jafferjees.com (homepage/About/blog, product/collection pages, FAQ/policy pages), run 2026-11-08. This documents what Jafferjees *already* says about itself, as input into the brand voice guide's "words we use" section.

## Heritage/craft words — homepage, About Us, blog

| Word/phrase | Where used |
|---|---|
| "Handcrafted" / "craftsmanship" / "crafted" | Homepage title tag; [About Us](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/about-us) ("care about every stitch, every detail"); [Sustainability page](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/sustainable-business-practices) ("true luxury goes beyond impeccable craftsmanship") |
| "Legacy" | [About Us](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/about-us): "built on a legacy of quality craftsmanship for over 140 years, now in the hands of the fifth generation" |
| "Fifth generation" / family language | [About Us](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/about-us): founder Noorbhoy Jafferjees, 1935 earthquake rebuild, commitment that "never wavered" |
| "Quality" | About Us; homepage ("meticulously crafted accessories," "refined craftsmanship") |
| "Clean" / "natural" / "consistent" / "honest" / "steady" | Blog posts (belts guide: "clean and consistent"; wallet guide: "clean," "natural," "consistent," "honest," "calm and consistent") |
| "Timeless" | About Us: "timeless designs ... meant to last a lifetime" |
| "Elegance" / "refined" | Homepage: "blend elegance with functionality," "refined craftsmanship" |
| "Complimentary" | Homepage services list (gift wrap, engraving) |

**Mini-taglines already in use:**
- "Jafferjees Handcrafted Genuine Leather Products in Pakistan, UK & USA" (homepage title)
- "built on a legacy of quality craftsmanship for over 140 years, now in the hands of the fifth generation" (About Us)
- "true luxury goes beyond impeccable craftsmanship" (Sustainability page opener)

Note: the Sustainability page notably avoids the word "heritage," using "luxury," "responsibly," "fair wages," "community" instead.

## Policy/service words — FAQ, Terms & Conditions, Corporate Queries

| Word/phrase | Where used |
|---|---|
| "Working days" | [FAQ](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/frequently-asked-question) ("ship the order within 2-3 working days"); [T&Cs](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) ("allow up to a maximum of 15 working days for deliveries") — never a hard date |
| "Personalized" / "personalization" | FAQ, T&Cs — used instead of "customized" |
| "Personalization services are provided without any additional cost" | FAQ — their phrase for complimentary embossing, not "free" |
| "Personalized items are not eligible for return or exchange under any circumstances" | T&Cs — exact no-refund wording for personalized goods |
| "No-refund policy" / "exchange" | T&Cs — "exchange" is the only remedy used, never "return" or "refund" |
| "Exceptional cases" | T&Cs — hedge language introducing narrow exchange exceptions (damaged, wrong item, personalization mismatch) |
| "Within three working days of receiving the parcel" | T&Cs — damage/mismatch reporting window, paired with "invoice and photos" |
| "We would love to hear from you" / "drop us a line" | [Corporate Queries](https://usa.jafferjees.com/pages/corporate-queries) — casual direct-address phrasing |

Tone: plain, direct, procedural — closer to a terms document than the craftsman voice on About Us. Polite but businesslike; always routes customers to email/WhatsApp rather than promising outcomes.

## Product-copy words — collection/product pages

| Word/phrase | Where used |
|---|---|
| "DETAILS: / FEATURES: / DIMENSIONS:" | Every product page checked (HKG Backpack, Park Lane Document Case, Geneva Wallet, Monaco Wallet, Basel Belt, Wall Street Executive Bag) — fixed spec-sheet structure |
| "Style & practicality" / "elegant design" / "refined professional style" | Recurring style/practicality pairing across product descriptions |
| "Best quality leather" / "premium leather" | Geneva Wallet, Park Lane Document Case |
| "A must-have for your collection" | Basel Belt |

**Naming system** (the one place a distinctive voice shows up consistently):
- Travel bags: airport codes — HKG, ZRH, MUC, DOH
- Document cases/briefcases: famous streets — Park Lane, Regent Street, Wall Street, Abbey Road, etc.
- Wallets: world cities — Monaco, Venice, Geneva, Karachi, etc.
- Belts: European towns — Mantova, Siena, Basel, etc.
- Dinner & Dance: flower names — Rosella, Freesia, Orchid, etc.

## Summary: the gap

All three passes independently flagged the same thing: the heritage/craft vocabulary ("legacy," "fifth generation," "handcrafted," "timeless") lives almost entirely on the About Us page. Product pages and collection pages — the pages customers spend the most time on — read like plain spec sheets with no heritage language at all. This matches the earlier finding in [`order-20464-voice-findings.md`](./order-20464-voice-findings.md): the brand voice is strong where it exists, but doesn't carry through to the pages and emails customers actually interact with most.

## Candidate words for "words we use" in the voice guide

From Jafferjees' own vocabulary: **handcrafted, legacy, fifth generation, timeless, complimentary, clean, honest, refined**.

Combined with the competitor-gap words already flagged as safe to adopt (see [`competitor-vocabulary-gap.md`](./competitor-vocabulary-gap.md)): **passed down, patina, named-artisan detail, repair over replace, full-grain, hand-stained/hand-inspected**.

Still flagged as unsafe to use until a real policy exists: **warranty, guarantee, lifetime** (no such policy is published).
