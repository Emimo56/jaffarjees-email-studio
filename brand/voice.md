# Jafferjees — Brand Voice Guide

## Our voice in three words
Pure. Heritage. Composed.

(Pure — real, natural leather, never synthetic or cheap. Heritage — since 1880, fifth generation. Composed — confident and calm, never shouting or over-apologizing.)

## Words we use
- Handcrafted, hand-stained, vegetable-tanned — name the actual process, not vague quality claims
- Since 1880, fifth generation, heritage, passed down — real history, not vague "tradition"
- Every stitch, every detail, grain, finish — sensory, tactile specifics
- Artisan, atelier, workshop — framed as skilled, ongoing practice
- Genuine, natural leather — proof-backed, not just asserted
- Restoration, care, lasts a lifetime — longevity and stewardship, not disposability
- Warm, direct, specific customer-service language: "here's what we can do," "we'll review this with you"

## Words we avoid
- "100% genuine leather" as a headline claim (reads as a red flag, not reassurance)
- "Affordable," "great deal," "buy now and save" — bargain language that undercuts premium positioning
- "Premium quality," "durable and long-lasting" used alone with no detail behind them
- "Perfect for everyday use," "great gift for any occasion" — generic filler used on every SKU
- Exclamation points, ALL CAPS, urgency ("LIMITED STOCK!")
- "Team Jafferjees," "Best," "Regards" — too generic/corporate for a family heritage house
- Anything implying refunds are freely available

## Claims we must never make
- Never promise a refund — our policy is strict No Refund; only exchanges, and only in exceptional cases (damaged item, wrong item, engraving mismatch), reported within 3 working days with invoice and photos
- Never promise an exact color/shade match — leather is natural and color varies by screen and by hide; frame this as proof of authenticity, not a flaw to apologize for
- Never claim same-day or guaranteed delivery dates — international delivery can take up to 15 working days
- Never claim a product is "vegan leather," "eco-leather," or any synthetic-adjacent term — our products are genuine, natural leather only
- Never overstate sustainability work beyond what's documented (fair wages, eco-conscious sourcing, Pakistan community initiatives) — no vague "100% sustainable" claims

## How we sign off
- Sign as **"The Jafferjees Family"** (or "Jafferjees, since 1880") — never "Jafferjees Store" or "Team Jafferjees"
- One warm, specific line before the sign-off — e.g. "Crafted by hand, cared for the same way — reach out any time." Not "Thank you for your purchase!"
- Keep the same sign-off phrase across channels — email and the note tucked into gift-wrapped orders should feel like the same hand wrote both
- No corporate closings ("Best," "Regards," "Team Jafferjees")
