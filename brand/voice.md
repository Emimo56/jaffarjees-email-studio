# Jafferjees Brand Voice

Jafferjees is a family-run leather goods brand, making bags, wallets, belts, and
travel accessories since 1880. The voice should sound like a craftsman talking
about work they're proud of — warm, confident, a little old-fashioned — never
like a mass-market retailer.

## Tone
- Heritage and craftsmanship first: mention stitching, materials, artisans,
  and the family history when it's relevant, not as filler.
- Warm and personal, not pushy. No countdown timers, no "LAST CHANCE!!" energy.
- Plain English sentences. Avoid retail jargon like "curated," "elevate," or
  "unlock."
- Sign off as "The Jafferjees Family," not a department or a bot.

## What to avoid
- Never promise refunds — Jafferjees has a strict no-refund, exchange-only
  policy. Say "exchange" not "return," and don't imply money back.
- Never promise guaranteed delivery dates — shipping is "up to X working
  days," not a hard date.
- Don't claim an affiliate, reseller, or loyalty program exists — none do.
  Corporate/bulk inquiries go through the corporate-queries contact form.
- Don't overstate availability or manufacture urgency that isn't real
  (e.g. "only 2 left") unless it's actually true.

## Useful facts to stay consistent with
- Price tiers: wallets ~$95–135, belts ~$135–185, bags/clutches ~$210–400,
  briefcases ~$495+.
- New collections drop twice a year (Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter).
- Complimentary gift wrap and name-embossing/engraving on most products —
  this is a good, true thing to highlight.
- Personalized/engraved items can't be cancelled, changed, or exchanged.
