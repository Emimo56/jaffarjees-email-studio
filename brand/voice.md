# Jafferjees Brand Voice

Jafferjees is a family-run leather goods brand, making bags, wallets, belts, and
travel accessories since 1880. The voice should sound like a craftsman talking
about work they're proud of — warm, confident, a little old-fashioned — never
like a mass-market retailer.

## Our voice in three words
Authentic. Reliable. Not cheap at all — premium, and priced like it.

## Tone
- Heritage and craftsmanship first: mention stitching, materials, artisans,
  and the family history when it's relevant, not as filler.
- Warm and personal, not pushy. No countdown timers, no "LAST CHANCE!!" energy.
- Plain English sentences.
- Sign off as "The Jafferjees Family" for newsletters and campaigns — not a
  department or a bot. For one-to-one service emails (order issues,
  questions, complaints), sign off as "Jafferjees Customer Care" instead —
  warmer than a generic department, but no invented personal names.

## Words we use
Our own words, already in use on the site and worth repeating consistently:
handcrafted, legacy, fifth generation, timeless, complimentary, clean, honest,
refined, exchange, personalized, working days.

Words competitors use that we don't, but fit us honestly and don't conflict
with any rule below: passed down, patina (wear as proof of use), named-artisan
or named-place detail (e.g. the actual 1935 Karachi rebuild, not vague quality
claims), repair over replace, full-grain, hand-stained / hand-inspected.
(Source: `findings/jafferjees-own-vocabulary.md` and
`findings/competitor-vocabulary-gap.md`.)

## What to avoid
- **The governing rule: only claim what's actually documented** — stated on
  the live site, or confirmed directly by the business (like "made in
  Pakistan," which the site itself doesn't say). Never infer, extrapolate,
  or invent a claim beyond that, no matter how plausible it sounds.
- **"Warranty," "guarantee," "lifetime"** — competitors use these heavily, but
  we have no published warranty policy. Don't borrow the word until there's a
  real policy behind it — it would be a false claim.
- Retail jargon like "curated," "elevate," or "unlock."
- Never promise refunds — Jafferjees has a strict no-refund, exchange-only
  policy. Say "exchange" not "return," and don't imply money back.
- Never promise guaranteed delivery dates — shipping is "up to X working
  days," not a hard date.
- Don't claim an affiliate, reseller, or loyalty program exists — none do.
  Corporate/bulk inquiries go through the corporate-queries contact form.
- Don't overstate availability or manufacture urgency that isn't real
  (e.g. "only 2 left") unless it's actually true.
- Never say "handmade" — our own word is "handcrafted."
- Never cite "140 years" — it's a stale rounding on the site itself; say
  "since 1880" / "fifth generation" instead.
- Never name or compare ourselves to a competitor.
- Before any email references a discount, sale, or "no current sale," check
  the live site first — pricing/promotions can change without us knowing.
  See `findings/site-inconsistencies.md`.
- Before any email promises a delivery-by date ("order by X to receive in
  time"), check current processing and shipping times on the live site/FAQ
  first — don't assume prior numbers still hold. Last checked 2026-11-08:
  ~2–3 working days processing + up to 15 working days delivery; exchanges
  (not refunds) must be requested within 3 working days of receipt;
  personalized/engraved items are never exchangeable. Re-verify before each
  send, the same as the discount/sale check above.

## Useful facts to stay consistent with
- Made in Pakistan; sold in Pakistan, UK, and USA. (The website itself
  doesn't state country of manufacture — this came directly from the
  business, not the site.)
- Price tiers: wallets ~$80–110, belts ~$120–185, bags/clutches ~$170–340,
  briefcases ~$495+. (Corrected 2026-11-08 by checking every item on the
  live Wallets, Belts, Women, and Work collection pages at
  usa.jafferjees.com, not just the New Arrivals SKUs — the earlier ranges
  were stale across all four categories except briefcases.)
- New collections drop twice a year (Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter).
- Complimentary gift wrap and name-embossing/engraving on most products —
  this is a good, true thing to highlight.
- Personalized/engraved items can't be cancelled, changed, or exchanged.
