findings docs. Verdicts below; items still needing a human check are listed separately in
[notes/pending-confirmations.md](../notes/pending-confirmations.md) rather than guessed at here.

   **safe to keep as written.** These gesture at "patina" without claiming it as a specific
   material/tanning process for these nine SKUs, which is the right call per voice.md's
   governing rule (only claim what's documented). Don't add "patina," "full-grain," or
   "hand-stained" without tannery confirmation.
2. **"Fifth generation now" (Men's Launch)** — **safe to keep.** Appearing once across all nine
   emails satisfies the "use sparingly, not as filler" rule; no need to add it elsewhere.
3. **Noir Clutch described as "sized for an evening out" (Women's Spotlight)** — **safe to
   keep.** Generic occasion language, not a claim about an unconfirmed named collection.
4. **Naxos/Capri contents** — matches the campaign brief's confirmed product-page description.
   Needs a quick re-check against the live product pages before send; see
   notes/pending-confirmations.md.
5. **Pricing** — matches the brief's table, checked against the live site on 2026-11-08. Needs
   re-verification at send time if time has passed, per the new delivery/pricing re-check rule in
   brand/voice.md; see notes/pending-confirmations.md.
6. **"Order by Nov 23" framing** — the date math and Thanksgiving padding are sound, but the
   cutoff itself is still a recommendation pending sign-off; see
   notes/pending-confirmations.md.
