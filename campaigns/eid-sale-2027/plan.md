# Eid Sale 2027 — Re-engagement Campaign Plan

**Status:** Planning draft only. No real sends, no real customer lists. All names, numbers, and lists below are placeholders for planning purposes.

## 1. Campaign Overview

- **Campaign name:** "Welcome Back for Eid" — Jafferjees Eid Sale Re-engagement
- **Summary:** A 4-week, email-only sequence inviting past Jafferjees customers back to shop new arrivals and Eid gifting pieces before Eid-ul-Fitr 2027.
- **Primary objective:** Re-engage past customers and drive them to the sale collection page, with a planning benchmark of 35% open rate and 8% click-through across the sequence.
- **Secondary objectives:**
  - Remind past customers of complimentary gift wrapping and engraving ahead of Eid gifting.
  - Introduce new arrivals (handbags, clutches, wallets, keyrings) to customers who last bought a different category.
  - Surface restoration services as a reason to re-engage with pieces customers already own.

## 2. Target Audience

Past Jafferjees customers only. Drafts and plans only — this plan never sends to or names a real person.

| Segment | Description | Focus Products |
|---|---|---|
| Men | Past customers who bought men's accessories | Windsor wallet, Drift Wallet, Ascent Card Case, belts, cufflinks |
| Women | Past customers who bought women's accessories | Horizon Handbag, Noir Clutch, Orbit Handbag |
| All past customers | Everyone who has purchased before, regardless of category | Gift sets, New Arrivals, The Art of Gifting collection |

- **Pain points and motivations:** Want a gift that feels personal and lasts, without the stress of guessing size, colour, or whether it will arrive on time; value genuine materials over flashy branding.
- **Where they spend time:** Email (their primary channel with us), their own inbox around gifting occasions.
- **Buying stage:** Consideration to decision — they already know and trust the brand from a past purchase; this sequence moves them from "aware of the sale" to "added something to cart."

## 3. Key Messages

- **Core message:** The pieces you already trust us for — now in new styles, ready for Eid gifting.
- **Supporting messages:**
  1. New arrivals this season, handcrafted the same way as the piece you bought before.
  2. Gifting is easier with complimentary gift wrapping and complimentary engraving — no extra charge, no extra errand.
  3. Already own a Jafferjees piece? Our restoration service keeps it looking the way it did the day you bought it.
  4. Every piece is genuine, natural leather — grain and shade vary hide to hide, which is part of what makes it real.
- **Message variations by segment:**
  - Men: lead with wallets, belts, cufflinks, card cases — practical, everyday-carry framing.
  - Women: lead with handbags and clutches — framing around craftsmanship and detail.
  - All past customers: lead with gift sets and The Art of Gifting — framing around giving, not just buying.
- **Proof points:** Since 1880, fifth generation, handcrafted, vegetable-tanned leather, every stitch done by hand.

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

- **Risk:** Sale collection page not ready before Email 3 send date.
  **Mitigation:** Confirm the page is live and linked at least 3 days before the Week 3 email is scheduled.
- **Risk:** Customers near Eid expect fast delivery and are disappointed by timing.
  **Mitigation:** Email 4 sets expectations clearly — international delivery can take up to 15 working days — so customers order with enough lead time.
- **Risk:** Segment lists overlap (a past customer could fit more than one group) and gets duplicate or conflicting emails.
  **Mitigation:** When a real list exists, assign each customer to one segment only, with "All past customers" as the fallback for anyone who doesn't clearly fit Men or Women.
