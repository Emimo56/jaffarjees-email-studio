# Campaign Brief: New Arrivals — Christmas Gifting Wave

Status: Draft plan only. Nothing in this campaign is scheduled or sent.
Verified against usa.jafferjees.com on 2026-11-08 (see Verification Notes).

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** "New Arrivals for the Holidays"

**Summary:** Introduce the 9-piece New Arrivals collection to the email list ahead of Christmas, segmented by Men's, Women's, and Gifting interest — full price, no discount, no paid media.

**Primary objective:** Drive email-attributed New Arrivals purchases within the order-by window.

**Secondary objectives:**
- Build awareness of complimentary gift wrap and engraving as a differentiator.
- Keep list engagement healthy (open/click) without resorting to discounting or false urgency.

## 2. Target Audience

Three segments:

| Segment | Products featured | Framing |
|---|---|---|
| Men's | Drift Wallet ($85), Ember Wallet ($85), Ascent Card Case ($85), Roam Keyring ($85) | Everyday carry, craftsmanship |
| Women's | Horizon Handbag ($260), Orbit Handbag ($170), Noir Clutch ($185) | New silhouettes, craftsmanship |
| Gifting (either gender) | All 9 items, incl. Naxos ($80) and Capri ($65) | Complimentary gift wrap + engraving, no guesswork |

**Note on Naxos and Capri:** these aren't in your Men's/Women's lists, and checking the product pages, they're actually pre-built gift sets the site itself categorizes under "Gift Sets" — Naxos is labeled "a gift solution for him" (wallet + card case + keychain) and Capri "a gift solution for her" (wallet + keychain). They fit the Gifting segment cleanly as you specified. If you'd also like Naxos offered to the Men's segment and Capri to the Women's segment as an add-on gift-set option, that's an easy addition — flagging it as optional rather than assuming it.

**Buying stage:** Consideration → decision. This list already knows the brand; the campaign's job is to prompt a purchase ahead of Christmas, not to introduce Jafferjees.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** New arrivals, handcrafted and ready for gifting — with complimentary gift wrap and engraving, and enough lead time to arrive before Christmas if you order in time.

**Supporting messages:**
1. Heritage/craft — fifth-generation handcrafted leather, since 1880 (use sparingly, where relevant, not as filler).
2. Gifting is handled for you — complimentary gift wrap and personalized engraving, no extra cost, no guesswork.
3. Honest timing — "order by [date] to receive in time" tied to "up to 15 working days" shipping, never a guaranteed delivery date.
4. Segment relevance — Men's everyday-carry pieces, Women's new silhouettes, or a ready-made gift that works for either.

**Hard copy rules (from brand/voice.md and this brief):**
- Never "handmade" — say handcrafted.
- Never warranty/guarantee/lifetime — no published policy exists.
- Never "return" or implied refund — say exchange; Jafferjees is no-refund, exchange-only, and personalized/engraved items can't be exchanged at all.
- Never a guaranteed delivery date — "order by X to receive in time," tied to "up to 15 working days."
- No invented urgency/scarcity ("only 2 left") unless genuinely true.
- No religion assumptions — the Dec 25 shipping deadline is a calendar fact to write to, not an assumption that every recipient celebrates it.
- Sign off as "The Jafferjees Family."
- No discount/sale language.

## 4. Channel Strategy

Email only, per budget. Three segment lists/tags, sent in parallel. No paid, no new content formats — reuse existing product photography from the New Arrivals collection page.

## 5. Content Calendar

Launch now: today is Nov 8, 2026, already inside the "early-to-mid November" start window.

Delivery math, from the live site's Terms & Conditions (not the FAQ page — see Verification Notes): 2–3 working days processing + up to 15 working days shipping + 3 working-day exchange window = **21 working days** of lead time needed.

Counting back 21 working days from Dec 24 (the day before Christmas, so an exchange issue can still be resolved) lands on **Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026**. Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26) falls inside this window and could slow courier/processing, so I'd market a cutoff a few days earlier than the strict calculation — recommend **Monday, Nov 23, 2026** as the stated "order by" date, not Nov 25.

| Week | Send date | Content | Segments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. Budget Allocation

Small, email-only. No paid media line. Spend is internal time: copywriting, light design, brand-review, and ESP sending cost for 9 emails across 3 segments. No contingency needed beyond normal review time.

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

1. **Launch timing is tight.** Today (Nov 8) is already inside the "early-to-mid November" start window. Mitigation: launch Week 1 immediately — don't wait on asset polish.
2. **Thanksgiving (Nov 26) sits inside the shipping window.** Mitigation: market Nov 23 as the cutoff, not the strict Nov 25 calculation.
3. **Overlapping segments.** Gifting-segment subscribers may also be tagged Men's or Women's. Mitigation: dedupe/suppress so no one gets 3 near-identical emails in the same week.
4. **Delivery-date overpromising.** Any drafted copy that states a firm delivery date instead of "order by X" phrasing should be caught in brand-review before send.
