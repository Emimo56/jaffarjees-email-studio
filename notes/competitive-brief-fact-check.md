## Summary of confirmed errors to fix in the original brief

1. Fossil's S&P/Moody's "Aug 2025 CC downgrade" — fabricated/misdated; remove or replace with the real Nov-2025 S&P distressed-exchange action.
2. "Reviews.io 3.0/5 from 26 reviews" (Fossil) — unfindable, remove.
3. "$100 custom-monogram-stamp fee" (Cuyana) — unsupported; actual range is $15–$498.
4. "5-year limited warranty" (Shinola) — wrong; Shinola's actual policy is a lifetime/7-year warranty.
5. "No personalization/monogramming found anywhere" (Saddleback) — contradicted by Saddleback's own FAQ.
6. American Airlines / US Navy as named Saddleback clients — unconfirmed in any source.
7. "No blog/journal/content hub" (Jafferjees) — a blog exists at `/blogs/usa-blogs`.
8. "No sustainability/ethical-sourcing narrative" (Jafferjees) — a "Sustainable Business Practices" page exists.
9. "Jetsetter" naming (Jafferjees) — the actual collection is branded "Travel."
10. 69% transparency-trust stat (MyTotalRetail/Flow.io) — unfindable, likely fabricated.
11. BNPL stat sourced to `btng-studio.pages.dev` — unverifiable/low-quality domain.
12. Brand-story willingness-to-pay stat — correct number, wrong journal/author citation (should be Lundqvist et al. 2013, *Journal of Brand Management*).

Everything else checked — including the hardest, most checkable facts (Trustpilot scores, BBB statuses, the FTC action against Shinola, the Bosca "Made in China" contradiction, and Saddleback's self-contradicting FAQ) — held up against live sources.
