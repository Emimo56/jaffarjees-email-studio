# Angle handoff — Email 01 subject lines

- Men's cares about: everyday-carry practicality, understated craftsmanship.
- Women's cares about: new silhouettes, clean/fresh design.
- Gifting cares about: gift problem solved (sets + complimentary wrap/engraving).

**Angle A — calm daily ritual:** fits Men's well (daily-use framing); weaker for Women's (new silhouettes aren't a "ritual") and Gifting (not about giving).

**Angle B — discovery of new arrivals:** generic "new pieces" framing that works across all three segments without forcing a mismatched angle.

**Pick: Angle B.** It's segment-neutral, so one subject-line style can flex across Men's/Women's/Gifting without the ritual framing feeling off for two of the three groups.
